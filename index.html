01About me

Hi, I'm Anirudh. My job is making sure the racks behind modern AI actually work when someone flips them on. At Quanta, I take NVIDIA GB300 and VR200 rack platforms and OCP servers for Meta's AI infrastructure through NPI: the stretch between “it powers on” and “great, now build ten thousand of them.”

Most days I'm somewhere between the signal and the system: ASIC-based rack validation, RDMA/RoCE networking, PRBS/SerDes signal integrity, BMC firmware (Redfish, IPMI), BIOS, CPLD, PCIe and NVMe debug, all the way up to L10–L12 rack bring-up. If I have to do something twice, I write a Python or Bash script so there's no third time.

I took the scenic route to hardware: Kubernetes platforms at Unisys, streaming pipelines at DLL, production RAG and ML at ResMed. So when a “hardware bug” turns out to be a software bug (or the other way around), I'm happy either way.

I'm also doing an executive PhD on the side, because one full-time job felt a little quiet.
