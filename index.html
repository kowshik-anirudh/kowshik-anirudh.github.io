About

I validate the AI racks that training and inference run on.

I'm a systems validation engineer at Quanta Manufacturing in Fremont, California. I've taken next-generation AI racks and OCP server platforms through NPI: bringing racks up, verifying their GPU and RDMA/RoCE fabrics, hardening BMC firmware, and tracing link failures down to PCIe and SerDes. Large AI jobs depend on every GPU and link in a rack working correctly, and validation is where problems get caught before they reach a production cluster.

I came to hardware through software: production Kubernetes on AKS and OpenShift at Unisys, Kafka-based data pipelines into Azure at DLL, then a production RAG assistant at ResMed. That range helps in validation. When a failure crosses the line between hardware, firmware and software, I can follow it across instead of handing it off, and I turn repetitive checks into Python and Bash automation so results stay repeatable from build to build.

Next, I want to keep building in AI infrastructure: rack-scale systems, GPU fabrics and the reliability of the clusters built from them. Alongside my job, I'm pursuing an executive PhD in Information Technology, with a concentration in Computer Engineering, at the University of the Cumberlands.
