About

I validate the racks modern AI runs on. Then I try to break them before anyone else can.

My job is the stretch between “it boots” and “ship it at scale.” At Quanta I've taken NVIDIA GB300 and VR200 AI racks and Meta OCP server platforms through NPI, and validated custom ASIC-based racks through EVT, DVT and PVT up to mass production.

I work from the signal up. I PXE-boot multi-host racks for L10–L12 bring-up, verify RDMA/RoCE across the rack, chase NVLink switch fabric issues with NVIDIA Fabric Manager, and isolate PCIe, NIC, NVMe and retimer link failures with PRBS/SerDes testing. If I do something twice, I script it. If the team does it twice, it goes in the framework.

I took the scenic route to hardware. Production Kubernetes at Unisys, data pipelines at DLL, a production RAG assistant at ResMed. So when a “hardware bug” turns out to be software, or the other way round, I don't need a handoff.

I'm also doing an executive PhD in Information Technology (Computer Engineering) at the University of the Cumberlands, because one full-time job felt quiet.
