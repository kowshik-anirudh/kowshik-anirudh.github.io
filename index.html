01About me

I'm a Systems Validation Engineer at Quanta Manufacturing in Fremont, working in AI infrastructure. I take AI server racks through NPI before mass production: NVIDIA GB300 and VR200 rack platforms, and OCP server platforms for Meta's AI infrastructure.

My work covers ASIC-based rack validation, RDMA/RoCE networking, PRBS/SerDes signal integrity, BMC firmware (Redfish, IPMI), and BIOS, CPLD, PCIe and NVMe debug, through to L10–L12 rack bring-up. I automate as much of it as I can with Python and Bash.

Before hardware, I worked across the software stack: production RAG and ML as a data scientist, streaming pipelines as a data engineer, and Kubernetes platforms as a software engineer. That background helps when a rack problem turns out to be a software problem, or the other way around.
